fashionistas.ai Crosslister

Read this first. This extension fills sell forms in your own logged-in tabs on Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Facebook, Grailed, Vinted, eBay and Etsy. It never sees or stores your passwords. Some of these sites' terms restrict automated tools, and accounts that use them can be flagged or limited. That's the same risk Vendoo and List Perfectly users take. You stay in control: by default you review every form and press the site's own Submit.
