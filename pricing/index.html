Billing questions

Is there a free plan? Yes. The free tier keeps 10 closet items, 5 crosslisted items and 3 try-ons a month, and it stays free — Pro is an upgrade you choose, not a trial that expires.

How do I cancel? Use the billing portal (POST /api/billing/portal from the app, or ask us from the contact page). Cancelling moves the account back to the free tier at the end of the paid period.

What does Stripe see? Stripe processes the card and the recurring $14.99 charge. fashionistas.ai stores your plan state, never your card number.
