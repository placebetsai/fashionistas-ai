Pricing

Free. Every part of it, while we're in launch.

No tiers, no listing fees, no cut of your sales. You keep what the marketplace lets you keep — the same take-home number you see on the fees calculator.

There is nothing to buy here. No plan, no card, no trial that turns into a charge. Checkout is not open and won't be until it says so on this page — everything you can click today, try-on included, costs nothing.

Launch

Free

No card, no trial, nothing to cancel. If any of that ever changes, it changes on this page first.

What's included

  • One Sell everywhere button per closet item
  • Shop-by-shop status. Nothing posts until you press Post on that shop
  • Real fee take-home shown as you price — not after you sell
  • Virtual try-on on your own photos — free while the site is in launch, including the photoreal render
  • Per-account daily limits, so a shop never sees a burst of listings from you

Already selling elsewhere? Photograph each piece and the fee take-home tells you which ones are worth moving first.

Straight answers

Do you take a cut of my sales? No. Your buyer pays the marketplace, the marketplace pays you. We never touch it.

Do you charge per listing? No — there is nothing to buy, so there is no plan to run up against. Each account does get a fair-use allowance of 10 listings, 3 AI photo rewrites and 10 stylist messages a month, because every AI call costs us money to run. It is a rate limit, not a paywall: it exists so one account cannot spend the whole budget, and there is no upgrade behind it.

Where do I cancel? There is nothing to cancel. The site is free while it is in launch, and there is no card on file.

Do you store my marketplace passwords? No. Never. Connecting a shop uses that shop's own sign-in, and the extension runs inside your browser.

Who is this for? Resellers and closet-clearers who are already listing by hand on more than one app.

Questions first? Contact us — a person answers.
