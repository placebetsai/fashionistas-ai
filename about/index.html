About fashionistas.ai

We help independent resellers clear a closet without rewriting the same listing six times.

What it is: an AI closet and resale studio. Snap a photo, get an item ID, a suggested price, and a fee take-home view, then copy paste-ready title and description drafts into Depop, eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Vinted or Grailed.

What it is not: an auto-poster. The app does not publish to marketplaces for you. You stay in control of every shop account and every post.

Who it is for: people selling their own clothes and accessories — including Etsy sellers who want one photo workflow across shops.

Where we are: Boca Raton, FL. Product email: fashionistas1979@gmail.com.

Stack: Cloudflare Pages + Workers. Your closet data stays on your account.
