About

Sell the same outfit six places without doing it six times.

Photograph your closet once. We identify it, price it, show what you actually keep — then publish it to every marketplace you're on with a single button.

What it is: an AI closet and resale studio. Snap a photo and you get an item ID, a suggested price, and the take-home figure after each marketplace's fee — so you know what a sale is actually worth before you list it. Then one Sell everywhere button writes and submits the listing on Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Vinted, Grailed, eBay, Etsy, Bonanza, Facebook Marketplace, Kidizen, Vestiaire Collective and Whatnot. Twelve shops, one photo, one click.

How posting works: connect each shop once from the Connect screen — one button per marketplace, then Connected ✓. After that, every closet item carries three choices: Try on, Style it, Sell it. Pressing Sell it posts on its own while you watch it happen, shop by shop: queued → posting → posted with a live link, failed with the reason and a Retry button, or needs_connection if a shop has timed out. Nothing is silently dropped and nothing is left half-written.

It sells, then it stops: the moment a sale lands, the item is pulled off every other shop within minutes. No double sales, no apologies to a second buyer, no delist marathon at midnight.

Try-on, both directions: buyers upload a photo and see themselves in the piece before they commit — sellers get the same engine to shoot better listing images. Everything the app suggests about how a garment sits, and how it pairs with what you already own, is computed from your photo, not from a stock library.

What it isn't: a middleman. We don't hold your money, we don't take a cut of your sale, and the shop feed links straight to the marketplace where the item is listed instead of routing checkout through us. Your shop logins stay yours — every connection is one you make yourself, and your closet data stays on your account.

Who it's for: people selling their own clothes and accessories — casual clear-outs, resellers running five shops at once, and Etsy sellers who want one photo workflow instead of six.

Where we are: Boca Raton, FL. Product email: fashionistas1979@gmail.com.

Stack: Cloudflare Pages + Workers.
