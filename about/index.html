About

Photograph it once. Sell it everywhere.

Photograph your closet once. We identify it, price it, show what you actually keep — then publish it to every marketplace you're on with a single button.

What it is: an AI closet and resale studio. Snap a photo and you get an item ID, a suggested price, and the take-home figure after each marketplace's fee — so you know what a sale is actually worth before you list it. Then one Sell everywhere button writes the listing for ten shops. Where a marketplace seller API is connected, the listing can be submitted that way. Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Vinted, Grailed, Facebook Marketplace, Vestiaire Collective and Whatnot expose no seller API, so the same button fills that listing inside your own signed-in browser tab — you never retype it. Kidizen shut down. Ten shops, one photo, one click.

How posting works: connect each shop once from the Connect screen — one button per marketplace, then Connected ✓. After that, every closet item carries a Try on control and a Sell everywhere button. Pressing Sell fills each shop while you watch it happen, shop by shop: queued → posting → posted with a live link, failed with the reason and a Retry button, or needs_connection if a shop has timed out. Nothing is silently dropped and nothing is left half-written.

When it sells: you take it down on each shop yourself. Nothing is pulled off eBay, Etsy, or anywhere else automatically, because those shops are not connected.

Try-on, both directions: buyers upload a photo and see themselves in the piece before they commit — sellers get the same engine to shoot better listing images. Everything the app suggests about how a garment sits, and how it pairs with what you already own, is computed from your photo, not from a stock library.

What it isn't: a middleman. We don't hold your money, we don't take a cut of your sale, and the shop feed links straight to the marketplace where the item is listed instead of routing checkout through us. Your shop logins stay yours — every connection is one you make yourself, and your closet data stays on your account.

Who it's for: people selling their own clothes and accessories — casual clear-outs, resellers running five shops at once, and Etsy sellers who want one photo workflow instead of six.

Where we are: Boca Raton, FL. Contact: our contact form.

Stack: Cloudflare Pages + Workers.

The short version

Most resale tools give you a text box and wish you luck. You photograph an item, and fashionistas.ai does the identifying, the pricing, the fee math and the posting — across ten shops. A connected seller API can submit the listing; the rest are filled in your own browser — and then helps you take it back down again when it sells. The work that used to be an evening is one button.
