Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026 (America/New_York). fashionistas.ai is a resale listing-draft tool. We do not sell your personal data.

What we collect

  • Account data — username, email, password hash, and closet listings you create (photos, titles, prices, notes).
  • Usage data — basic operational logs needed to run Cloudflare Workers / Pages (IP, timestamps, error traces).
  • Contact form — name, email and message you submit on /contact, delivered to fashionistas1979@gmail.com via FormSubmit.

What we do not do

  • We do not post to Depop, eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Vinted or Grailed on your behalf.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to data brokers.
  • We do not place Google AdSense ad units on empty app shells.

Where data lives

Application data is stored with Cloudflare (Workers / D1). Contact form messages are processed by FormSubmit and emailed to us.

Your choices

Email fashionistas1979@gmail.com to request account deletion or a copy of your data. Boca Raton, FL.

This page is a plain-language policy for the multilist draft product. It is not legal advice.
