Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026 (America/New_York). fashionistas.ai is a resale listing-draft tool. We do not sell your personal data.

What we collect

  • Account data — username, email, password hash, and closet listings you create (photos, titles, prices, notes).
  • Usage data — basic operational logs needed to run Cloudflare Workers / Pages (IP, timestamps, error traces).
  • Contact form — name, email and message you submit on /contact, forwarded by our server to a mail relay. The relay's own address is not published anywhere on this site.

What we do not do

  • Listing to eBay and Etsy runs through their own OAuth connection: we hold the connection and send the listing you approved.
  • For Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Vinted, Grailed, Facebook Marketplace, Vestiaire Collective and Whatnot there is no seller API. Nothing is sent to those shops — our browser extension types the draft into the form inside your own signed-in tab, and your session with that shop stays yours. Kidizen shut down, so it is not one of these shops.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to data brokers.
  • Advertising. Google AdSense is loaded on the marketing pages (home, about, pricing and the guide). Google and its partners may set cookies on those pages to serve and measure ads. Ad units are not placed on screens where you are working with your closet.

Where data lives

Application data is stored with Cloudflare (Workers / D1). Contact form messages are processed by FormSubmit and emailed to us. Virtual try-on renders are processed by our render provider, which receives the two images for that single render and nothing else. Fonts, the 3D model viewer and the on-device pose check are loaded from Google and jsDelivr CDNs, which see your IP address when they serve those files.

Your choices

Use the contact form to request account deletion or a copy of your data. Boca Raton, FL.

This page is a plain-language policy for the multilist draft product. It is not legal advice.
