Profile

The person behind fashionistas.ai: Israel Joffe

Israel Joffe is a senior project manager and executive in Boca Raton, Florida, and one of the people who builds fashionistas.ai — the photos, the listings, and the judgement that decides what a seller should see first.

What he brings to a marketplace

A marketplace lives or dies on its listing pages: the picture, the words under it, and how quickly a seller can get from a garment on a chair to something somebody else wants to buy. That is a delivery problem before it is a code problem, and it is the kind of problem Israel Joffe has spent his career on. He is a PMP #3371929 (Project Management Professional) and holds PMI-CPMAI #2509803, an AI Project Management Professional certification that expires 9/2/2028. His specialisms are AI-driven project delivery, fintech transformation and enterprise automation — the plumbing that decides whether a feature ships on the date it was promised.

He has helped build the federation's own products: CreateStuff.ai, Fashionistas.ai, PlaceBets.ai, MarketPicks.ai and IHateCollege.com.

A career spent looking at pictures

Before the project plans there was the media side. Israel Joffe's media and IT background runs through Fox 5 News, ABC News, Fox News, and Starwood / Marriott — newsrooms and hospitality groups where an image is judged in about a second. Muck Rack lists his work as seen in Newsweek, Yahoo, WNYW-TV, WTVT-TV and Greenwich Time.

That is the eye a resale app needs. When fashionistas.ai asks a seller for one photo and turns it into an item ID, a price and a listing, the questions being asked are the old editorial ones: is this frame clear, does it show the thing, and does it make someone stop scrolling. He also built and maintained websites and social media for WWE Hall of Famer Johnny Rodz at World of Unpredictable Wrestling in Brooklyn — years of posting, re-posting and keeping a feed alive, which is what a shop page is.

Where the rest of the résumé comes from

Federal service
Served the federal government at the FDIC and the FDA.
Roots
Born and raised in New York City and the Five Towns on Long Island. Moved to Washington, DC in 2016, then to the Palm Beach area in 2021.
Volunteer work
Volunteer firefighter with the Lawrence-Cedarhurst Fire Department (LCFD), where he served during Hurricane Sandy.
Martial arts
Second-degree black belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, promoted by UFC Hall of Famer Matt Serra. Trained for years under WWE Hall of Famer Johnny Rodz at World of Unpredictable Wrestling (WUW) in Brooklyn.

He is based in Boca Raton, Florida, and works on fashionistas.ai alongside the rest of the federation.
