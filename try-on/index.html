Get a better result

  • Stand straight with your whole torso in frame, facing the camera.
  • Plain background, even light — no harsh shadows across the body.
  • Lay the garment flat, or shoot it on a hanger, so the shape reads clearly.
  • Use the biggest photo you have. Small, blurry shots give small, blurry results.
