What happens when you press Try it on

The page sends a multipart POST with two parts — person and garment — to /api/tryon. The engine decodes both images, aligns the garment to the person, and returns the rendered image, which is drawn straight into the panel above. Nothing is posted anywhere else and nothing is published to a marketplace.
