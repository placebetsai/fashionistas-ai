Terms of Use

Effective October 12, 2026. These are the published terms for fashionistas.ai. The site is free: we do not charge you, there is no subscription, and we do not take a percentage of anything you sell.

1. What fashionistas.ai is

fashionistas.ai is a tool for people who resell clothing. It turns your photos and notes into listing drafts, shows what each marketplace would keep from a price, and can render an image of a garment on a person (a virtual try-on). It is software. It is not a marketplace: there are no buyers here, no checkout, and nothing is sold on this site.

2. It is free, and there is nothing to buy

There is no price, no card, no trial, no subscription, and no commission. We do not take a cut of anything you sell anywhere. The fee numbers the site shows you are each marketplace's own fees, computed from a published rate table so you can see your take-home before you list — none of that money is ours.

Each account does get a fair-use allowance, because every AI feature costs real money to run. Per account, per calendar month (UTC): 10 listing drafts, 3 AI photo rewrites, and 10 stylist messages. Virtual try-on is limited to 6 renders per account per day, resetting the next day. These are rate limits, not a plan: there is no upgrade behind them and nothing to buy when you hit one. If paid features are ever introduced, the price will be shown to you before you are charged and this page will carry a new date.

You sign up with an email address and a password. Your password is stored only as a salted password hash — we do not keep a readable copy of it. You are responsible for what happens under your account, so keep your password to yourself. You need to be old enough to enter a binding contract where you live to use the site.

4. Your content

You give us the permission needed to operate the service with them: storing them, showing them back to you, and processing them through the AI features you choose to run — for example, sending a photo pair to the rendering service when you press "try it on". Our privacy policy says exactly what is stored and where it goes. Listing drafts the site generates for you are yours to use.

5. AI output is a draft, not a fact

Text and images produced by the AI features are generated. They can be wrong: a draft may misstate a brand, condition, size, or the fees a marketplace charges, and a try-on image is an illustration of how a garment might look — not a photograph of you wearing it, and not a promise about fit. Read everything before you publish it anywhere. You decide what gets published; the site does not publish on your behalf (see below).

6. The marketplaces are other companies

What the site actually does with them today, stated plainly:

  • For the eight shops that have no seller API (Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Vinted, Grailed, Facebook Marketplace, Vestiaire Collective, Whatnot), publishing happens through a browser extension running inside your own signed-in browser session. That extension is not currently distributed for download, so today those shops are reached by you copying the draft in yourself.
  • eBay and Etsy have server-side connectors in the product, but no real listing has ever been posted through them. The eBay connector talks to eBay's sandbox environment, not eBay's production API, and the Etsy connector creates a draft.
