Install the Fashionistas extension

It posts your listings from your own browser, where you're already logged in to Poshmark, eBay, Depop and the rest. We never see your passwords and you never paste a key.

Download for Chrome
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Come back to your board — your shops are detected automatically.

Works on desktop Chrome, Edge and Brave. A one-click Chrome Web Store install is coming; until then this is the manual install. Phones can't run browser extensions — on your phone use “Post everywhere” step-by-step mode.
