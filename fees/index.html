Fee take-home

What you keep after marketplace fees

Before you paste a fashionistas.ai draft into Depop, eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Vinted or Grailed, check approximate seller take-home. Rates below mirror the fee concept in the app (platform % of sale price). Shipping and payment nuances vary by country — confirm on each shop before you price.

Vinted is modeled at 0% seller fee in most regions (buyers pay protection). Poshmark uses a flat $2.95 under $15 and 20% at $15+. Other % values are common US seller approximations used for take-home comparison — the live app refreshes platform fee % from its marketplace list after you sign in.

Why this page exists

fashionistas.ai prepares text drafts you post yourself. Fee clarity is part of that workflow: know net take-home before you paste. This calculator is static HTML for search and for sellers who want the comparison without opening the app shell.
