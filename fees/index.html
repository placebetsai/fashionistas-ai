What you keep after marketplace fees

Check what you actually keep before you list. fashionistas.ai shows this same take-home number on every item as you price it, so the fee never surprises you at the end. Rates below are platform % of sale price — shipping and payment nuances vary by country, so confirm on each shop before you price.

Rates verified against each marketplace's own published seller fees for 2026 (US). Poshmark is a flat $2.95 under $15 and 20% at $15+; Depop dropped its 10% commission in 2024 and now charges processing only; Vinted charges sellers 0% in most regions. Why this page exists

fashionistas.ai prices your closet and posts it to every shop you have connected — and it works out the take-home first. That is the point of this page: know your net before you commit to a price, not after the sale. This calculator is static HTML so you can compare marketplaces without opening the app.
